How we make apps

Every app is different. The promises are the same.

  • No ads, no tracking

    None of our apps show ads, run analytics or follow you around the web.

  • No account to make

    Open the app and start. There's nothing to sign up for and no password to forget.

  • Your things stay yours

    Photos, movies and recipes are kept on your device. Each privacy policy says in plain words what, if anything, leaves it.

  • People answer

    Write to us and you'll hear back from the people who build the apps.

Need help?

Each app's help page answers the common questions. If yours isn't there, write to us at support@calastudios.app. We read every message.
